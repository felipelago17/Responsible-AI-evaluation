# AI Governance & Evaluation — Daily Digest

Automated daily scan of regulators, research feeds (arXiv cs.AI/cs.CR/cs.CY), think tanks and frontier-lab announcements, filtered for AI safety, evaluation, red-teaming, governance and assurance. Items are unreviewed links — verify against the primary source before relying on them.

Updated automatically every day by the **Daily AI Governance Digest** GitHub Actions workflow.
Roughly 90 days of history are kept on this page; the latest run is also written to
`data/digest-latest.json`.
